Seed

The six rules

Where it is, is what it is about.

How far it reaches is how well you understand it.

How thick it is, is how often you have come back.

A cord with a bloom is a connection you made. Two colors means across fields.

Contours are what has settled. They never go away.

A dotted reach is a question. A dark stub is something you got wrong.
